# GCC/GCC-High Deployment

Agent Factory supports deployment to commercial and government environments:

- **commercial**: Standard endpoint (`api.agentfactory.com`).
- **gcc**: Federal platform (`api.gov.agentfactory.com`).
- **gcc-high**: Secure government (`api.high.gov.agentfactory.com`).
- **dod**: Department of Defense (`api.dod.agentfactory.mil`).
